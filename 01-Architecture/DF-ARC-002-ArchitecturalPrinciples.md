# 1. Introduction

Architectural principles define foundational design, evolution and maintenance contracts within their declared scope. They are long-term commitments applied through responsible sources, not evidence that every component or management system is implemented or conforms.

Architectural decisions shall identify applicable principles, owner contracts and actual evidence. Unmet criteria, conflicts and deviations remain explicit; this source supplies no automatic exception, approval or global correctness claim.

---

# 2. Purpose

The primary responsibility is foundational architectural design principles and decision constraints; therefore Type is Architecture. Family Architecture identifies the common documentary area, not a component or special Principles Type. Stable identity is independent of classification.

The principles support assessed conceptual integrity, coherent decision-making, incremental improvement and controlled stability. These are design objectives/requirements whose realization needs applicable contracts and scoped evidence; the document does not guarantee prevention of every future degradation.

---

# 3. Scope

Applicable principles concern logical documents/editions, Metadata and domain definitions, registered capability components, structural allocations, relationships and actual representations within their stated contexts. These are distinct entity kinds:documents or concerns do not become architectural components merely by appearing in the scope list. The supplied register has23 capabilities; Foundation/Architecture also provide support definitions.

Every README.md, CHANGELOG.md and ArchitecturalMilestones.md at every location is a non-normalized functional file. Normalization requires no Document ID, Family, Type, Component, Version, Revision, Status or Baseline for those names. Applicable content, organization, references, history and hash checks remain. This exclusion creates no exceptions to the four Types for normalized documents, and does not waive other actually applicable content contracts.

DF defines documentary contracts; DMS, tools or applicable manual processes execute document-management/checking operations. CMS governs changes in its own scope. Architectural definitions, explicit normative statements and source presence prove neither deployed services nor approval/publication/Baseline membership. Domain semantic owners retain their definitions; this common design source does not replace their detailed contracts or create missing Elements/Profiles.

# 5. Conceptual Principles

## 5.1. AP-001 — Documentation as Knowledge

Documentation shall represent organized knowledge through logical entities, identified sources and applicable contracts. A file is a representation, not automatically a registered capability or a complete knowledge system. Normalized sources and functional files contribute within their respective scopes; DF defines documentary requirements while an applicable process produces/manages representations.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess purpose, actual source organization, relationships and coverage. The principle creates neither a document component nor proof that every file contributes complete or consistent knowledge.

---

## 5.2. AP-002 — Explicit Architecture

Architectural decisions shall identify the subject, scope, responsible owners, assumptions, applicable contracts and rationale explicitly. Significant allocation, classification, identity or required-contract changes require previous/new mapping and affected editions before propagation. Record unresolved decisions or missing evidence rather than treating an implicit label, arrow or directory as authority.

**Evidence boundary:** Examine actual decision/owner/reference records and limits. Explicit documentation proves neither approval nor complete implementation; derived summaries preserve authoritative provenance.

---

## 5.3. AP-003 — Technology Independence

Architectural definitions shall avoid prescribing technology unless an explicit integration constraint requires it. Logical identity, classification and domain meaning are distinct from filenames, formats, repositories and storage technology. Actual implementations still require representations, adapters, interfaces and executors under their applicable contracts.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess declared technology constraints and actual representation/adapter compatibility. Conceptual independence does not guarantee every format/tool is suitable, an implementation exists or technologies can evolve without consumer impact.

---

## 5.4. AP-004 — Single Source of Truth

Each semantic concept or authoritative record shall have one identified responsible owner within its declared domain/scope. Metadata Dictionary defines descriptor meaning while Identity, Classification and Governance retain their domain semantics. Traceable summaries, catalogs and projections may repeat derived values with source-edition provenance and refresh/state limits without acquiring competing authority.

**Evidence boundary:** Check responsible definition/record sources, derived-copy provenance and unresolved conflicts. Logical single authority does not require one physical copy, a universal Registry route or a complete canonical catalog merely from this principle.

---

## 5.5. AP-005 — Semantic Consistency

Consuming representations shall preserve applicable owner meanings and identify subjects/qualified contexts. Controlled aliases and historic vocabulary retain explicit mappings; unqualified overloads or competing meanings are recorded and reconciled. Metadata represents domain values, Schema organizes references, Profile constrains usage and Instance provides values under one effective Profile without redefining semantics.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess actual definitions, profiles, represented values and consumer scope. This principle does not manufacture missing Element/Profile records or prove consistency of unseen sources.

# 6. Structural Principles

## 6.1. AP-006 — Separation of Responsibilities

Each registered capability shall have a defined primary semantic responsibility and primary allocation. Explicit collaboration, cross-cutting participation, shared check domains and traceable projections are permitted while preserving owner boundaries. Reviews may use Validation/Consistency Verification results without acquiring their rule ownership or approval authority.

**Evidence boundary:** Check primary owner/consumer contracts and actual support interactions. Prevent competing semantics rather than prohibiting every overlap of supporting work; no new capability or reassignment is inferred from a concern or directory.

---

## 6.2. AP-007 — Layered Architecture

Structural allocation shall follow Architectural Layers:seven areas are Foundation, Architecture, Documentation, Governance, Discovery, Automation and Integration. Foundation/Architecture supply supporting definitions; the supplied 23 capabilities retain their 4/4/5/5/5 allocation. Five conceptual concerns—Knowledge, Documentation, Governance, Discovery and Preservation—are a different view.

**Evidence boundary:** For required cross-Layer consumption, record consumer→provider contract/scope/source editions and apply the declared support order:the provider precedes its consumer. Same-Layer requirements, including Knowledge Graph→Relationships and Workflow→Policies, need explicit interfaces/ownership and cycle assessment. There is no obligation to consume every earlier provider or an adjacent serial service chain. Genuine required cycles are prohibited in the governed scope and remain visible for reconciliation; no projection/reference label hides them. Changing constraints requires assessed architectural revision, not an arbitrary upward exception. These rules certify neither a full graph nor implemented layer services.

---

## 6.3. AP-008 — Modular Design

Components shall preserve coherent boundaries and compatible provider contracts where feasible. Separate areas or documents may still require coordinated content/descriptor, owner/consumer or release changes. Independent evolution is an assessed objective under actual dependencies, not an unconditional guarantee.

**Evidence boundary:** Map affected exact editions, provider compatibility and transition evidence. A new component or module label alone proves neither independent release nor operational modularity.

---

## 6.4. AP-009 — Explicit Relationships

Applicable connections shall be explicitly typed with identified endpoints and provenance. Distinguish documentary authority references, semantic associations, representation/projection, navigation, required consumption and execution. A required edge names consumer, provider, contract, scope and responsible source editions; physical directories, names and visual order alone establish no edge.

**Evidence boundary:** Check actual connection records and resolvable endpoints. Preserve genuine requirements/cycles visibly and reconcile conflicting owner contracts; do not rename an indispensable provider as navigation to create apparent acyclicity. This principle imposes no nonempty relationship list on every file.

---

## 6.5. AP-010 — Stable Identity

Normalized documents shall preserve valid permanent logical identity under the applicable Domain+Identifier allocation/preservation contract. Identifier representation, current classification, title, Version/Revision, Status and physical location are distinct facts. Existing valid DF-SSS-NNN allocations do not automatically change with Family/Type or path; necessary migration needs a mapped assessed identity change.

**Evidence boundary:** Apply the functional-file exclusion in §3 first:README.md, CHANGELOG.md and ArchitecturalMilestones.md require no normalized ID by this principle. Assess actual uniqueness/resolution and collision evidence; no 000 functional identity, missing allocation or universal generation algorithm is invented.

# 7. Governance Principles

## 7.1. AP-011 — Lifecycle Governance

Lifecycle contracts shall identify their governed subject and responsible domain. Document-edition Status differs from Metadata Element, Classification-definition or Template lifecycle, maturity, logical identity, Version/Revision and snapshot membership. Applicable normalized documentary state/transition requirements remain explicit; functional names follow §3 without mandatory normalized lifecycle fields.

**Evidence boundary:** Workflow/Policies define documentary contracts; DMS/tool/manual processes execute operations and CMS governs changes in its own scope. A state list creates no universal permission graph. Review, validation, approval, publication and membership remain separately evidenced for exact editions; older approval does not approve Draft successors.

---

## 7.2. AP-012 — Traceability

Architectural decisions, source editions, typed relationships and actual changes shall retain appropriate identity/provenance/history evidence within the declared scope. Preserve received declarations, absent historic facts, actual attribution and previous/new mappings; do not rewrite pending or failed events as earlier successes.

**Evidence boundary:** Results identify examined records/bytes, coverage, executor and limits. Actual contribution, user authorization, review, validation, ownership and approval are separate. Later human/tool disk edits require resulting-byte assessment; previous results neither transfer automatically nor assign responsibility for consequences of a changed final product. No full traceability is claimed for unexamined history.

---

## 7.3. AP-013 — Controlled Evolution

Changes shall follow applicable owner/governance contracts with explicit rationale, affected source editions, decision/evidence requirements and transition mapping. DF defines documentary contracts; applicable processes execute authorized operations, while CMS supplies change evidence within its own scope. Domain rule ownership remains distinct from checking or generation.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess actual change records, required decisions and coverage. Authorization to edit is not approval/publication/Baseline selection; governance wording proves no installed workflow or complete transition model.

---

## 7.4. AP-014 — Baseline Preservation

A DF Baseline shall be separately identified and immutable as an explicit selection of exact approved document editions, with relevant pinned relationship context or traceable verification evidence. Member logical identity, edition coordinates, approval eligibility, consistency and actual inclusion are distinct. Immutability is a property of the selected snapshot, not a condition activated only by publication.

**Evidence boundary:** Preserve selection scope/time/evidence and exact member references. A mutable working collection is not certified as an immutable Baseline merely by its name. Draft successors do not retarget older members or inherit membership. A current path, time label, publication or successful check alone creates no Baseline; no missing historic approval is invented.

# 8. Evolution Principles

## 8.1. AP-015 — Incremental Evolution

Prefer incremental architectural improvement where it can meet actual requirements while preserving compatible meaning. A necessary larger or foundational change can be justified when incremental alternatives cannot adequately satisfy identified responsibilities or constraints.

**Evidence boundary:** Record previous/new contracts, alternatives/rationale, affected owners/consumers, dependencies and transition/verification evidence. Avoid disruption where feasible without an absolute ban on justified incompatible reconciliation.

---

## 8.2. AP-016 — Backward Compatibility

Preserve compatible meaning and provider/consumer contracts where feasible. Necessary incompatible changes shall be explicitly justified with affected exact editions, previous/new mappings, reference/identity effects, transition evidence and verification limits. Compatibility is assessed from contracts, not from names, folder layout or a Version label alone.

**Evidence boundary:** For this working document review, compatible correction retains Version; compatible additive capability increments minor; incompatible contract change increments major and resets minor to 0. Every changed persisted edition increments Revision monotonically across Version changes. Validation of unchanged source changes neither coordinate and is tracked centrally. Domain-specific entity numbering requires its own qualified owner; no completed consumer rollout is inferred.

---

## 8.3. AP-017 — Extensibility

Introduce a new capability or source role only for a demonstrated need with defined owner responsibility, primary allocation, typed interactions and consumer impact. Reuse or extend existing contracts where feasible. Necessary changes to foundations, responsibilities or interfaces are permitted through the assessed evolution process in AP-015/016 and §14; extension-only is not an absolute prohibition on foundational revision.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess whether existing meaning/functionality changes, actual dependencies and transition evidence. A new capability is not assumed harmless and need not create another Layer. No component, Metadata Element, Profile or specification is fabricated merely to make an extension appear complete.

---

## 8.4. AP-018 — Architectural Stability

Preserve coherent conceptual boundaries and compatible owner meanings where feasible. Implementation technologies can evolve under applicable representation/provider/consumer contracts; their independence is not guaranteed by conceptual separation. Necessary semantic changes retain source history and explicit compatibility/version transition.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess actual contracts, affected editions and integration/adapter constraints. Stability is a controlled design objective, not foundation immutability, harmless extension or a guarantee of unchanged semantics for every future edition.

# 9. Quality Principles

## 9.1. AP-019 — Simplicity

Select the simplest organization and contracts that satisfy identified responsibilities, applicability and required evidence. Do not omit genuine dependencies, owner boundaries or unresolved requirements merely to simplify a view.

**Evidence boundary:** Evaluate actual alternatives and constraints within scope. Simplicity does not justify hidden required edges, invented completeness or removal of historic evidence.

---

## 9.2. AP-020 — Consistency

Apply coherent responsibility-based patterns and preserve canonical domain meanings with explicit contexts/aliases. Type uses Architecture, Structure, Behavior or Reference for normalized documents; Family follows canonical component or admitted documentary-area membership independently of ID/path.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess actual source content, classification, representations and owner/consumer scope. Functional exclusions remain uniform; partial propagation and stale maps remain findings rather than presumed global consistency.

---

## 9.3. AP-021 — Maintainability

Architecture shall support traceable identities, scoped source/edition history, explicit references and controlled owner/consumer changes to facilitate maintenance. Representations and descriptive content may need coordinated updates under actual contracts.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess real maintenance work, affected references and executor/tool constraints. A directory structure, capability definition or automation goal proves neither automatic synchronization nor manageable maintenance for every future case.

---

## 9.4. AP-022 — Discoverability

Support access through actual resolving source maps and identified entity/edition views. Discovery, Navigation and Search expose declared sources with relevant state, provenance and current/stale/historical/snapshot context. Directories are physical organization, not competing semantic authority.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess actual targets, coverage and freshness. Discoverability is an intended assessed quality; it grants no approval and implies neither complete indexing nor a mandatory Registry pipeline.

---

## 9.5. AP-023 — Verifiability

Architectural requirements shall be stated so applicable checks can identify rules, exact artifacts/editions, relevant context and observations. Validation/Consistency Verification/Reviews consume responsible owner contracts and record executor, coverage, results and limits.

**Evidence boundary:** A scoped PASS closes only evidenced criteria for examined bytes; it does not approve, publish, select Baseline members, prove full required-graph consistency or transfer to later changed products. Missing rules, evidence or coverage remain explicit; principle compliance is not itself an implemented verifier.

---

## 9.6. AP-024 — Reusability

Design reusable capability/definition boundaries under applicable domain contracts, scope and constraints. Reuse preserves primary semantic ownership and traceable source meaning rather than creating competing copies or unrestricted adapters.

**Evidence boundary:** Assess suitability, compatibility and actual reuse evidence for intended consumers. Maximal reuse is not a guarantee of universal domain suitability, unrestricted scale, implemented services or technology-independent execution.
