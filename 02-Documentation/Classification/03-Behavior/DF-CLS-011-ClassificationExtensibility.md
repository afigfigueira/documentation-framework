# 1. Introduction

The Documentation Framework is designed to evolve over time.

As new documentation domains, document categories and architectural requirements emerge, the Classification component shall support controlled extension without compromising:

- architectural integrity;
- semantic consistency;
- structural consistency;
- compatibility;
- canonical Classification definitions.

Classification Extensibility defines controlled evolution:ordinary extension preserves applicable foundations;necessary assessed mandate revision explicitly changes identified prior requirements under actual responsible authority.

The Framework's broader architectural model establishes that an architecture is not considered permanently complete, but evolves incrementally while preserving its fundamental principles.

**Current governing scope.** Primary responsibility remains controlled extension/processes:Behavior/current03-Behavior under ReviewPolicy3.1/ARC0075/9,no move. Current membership Family,uniform primary Type and optional defined Class apply. Working Draft correction is not actual canonical adoption. Ordinary preservation and necessary assessed normative/foundational/role/interface/domain revision are distinguished under§79.

# 4. Extensibility Principles

Classification Extensibility follows the principles below.

---

## 4.1. Architectural Stability

Ordinary extensions shall preserve applicable Classification stability;necessary assessed mandate revision follows§79.

An extension shall not silently undermine foundations;any necessary foundational change requires explicit actual prior/new authority,need,impact,compatibility/history and transition evidence.

---

## 4.2. Semantic Preservation

Ordinary extensions shall preserve the established meaning of existing Classification Elements;necessary explicitly assessed semantic revision follows§79.

An extension shall not silently redefine an existing Family, Type or Class.

**Current governing scope.** No silent redefinition is mandatory. Necessary explicit revision identifies actual prior/new meaning,need,authority,alternatives,rationale,batch,compatibility/mappings/history,pinned continuity,authorized transition and later checks. Existing owner restrictions remain actual pending differences until separately reconciled;the word extension grants no permission.

---

## 4.3. Structural Preservation

Ordinary extensions shall remain compatible with applicable canonical Classification structure;necessary structural incompatibility is explicitly assessed and governed.

New elements shall occupy positions permitted by the Classification Schema.

**Current governing scope.** Actual Schema34 known duties versus genuine general multiplicity/field/population gaps remain. Documentary roles do not impose a universal tree;actual defined scheme positions/properties still require real authority. Necessary change does not leave simultaneous contradictory same-context canonical demands.

---

## 4.4. Backward Compatibility

Extensions should preserve the validity and interpretation of existing Classification whenever reasonably possible.

Existing documents should not become semantically ambiguous merely because Classification evolves.

**Current governing scope.** Interpretation,original validity,current compliance/use and retrieval differ. Preserve compatible ordinary use where feasible;necessary incompatibility must be explicit and governed for actual affected editions/consumers. New rules are not backdated and required history is not discretionary.

---

## 4.5. Canonical Preservation

Extensions shall remain part of one canonical Classification architecture.

Independent competing Classification models shall not be introduced as extensions.

**Current governing scope.** One canonical architecture is a requirement,not a complete populated accepted model or proof every proposal is canonical. Actual Drafts and scoped experiments may exist without competing canonical authority;changed successors inherit no earlier approval or exact Baseline membership.

---

## 4.6. Technology Independence

Extension mechanisms shall remain independent of:

- programming languages;
- databases;
- repositories;
- serialization formats;
- implementation frameworks.

**Current governing scope.** Meaning independence preserves actual owner duties for descriptor/Schema/effective Profile/Instance,physical placement,references,indexes,interfaces,access/retrieval/freshness,usage and required history. It does not prove installed capability or no effects from changing representation.

---

## 4.7. Controlled Evolution

Classification extensions shall be introduced through the applicable governance process.

Actual scoped approval is necessary for canonical extension;required conditions must be fulfilled and effective adoption evidenced under§§35/37/39/79.

**Current governing scope.** Necessary actual approval must identify proposal/subject/edition,scope,authority,criteria/evidence,rationale and conditions. Actual required fulfillment,effective adoption/activation and separate publication/Baseline claims follow§§34–39. CONTINUAR authorizes this working phase,not canonical permission.

# 7. Extensible Classification Elements

The following Classification artifacts may be extended.

---

## 7.1. Classification Families

An actual Family membership option may be introduced only for a real canonical component or admitted documentary area under responsible authority;it is not automatic allocation of a new component or Type-owning high-level domain.

An existing Family membership meaning shall not be silently redefined;necessary assessed revision requires the actual prior/new authority and continuity evidence in§79.

**Current governing scope.** Membership options require actual component/admitted-area existence and authorization;directory alias,role label or a Family A/B/C picture creates no component,key,series,population or new primary Type. Actual scope/definition/history remains identifiable.

---

## 7.2. Classification Types

Primary document Type is exactly one uniform Architecture,Structure,Behavior or Reference responsibility across Families;ordinary extension does not add Family-owned Types or a fifth primary responsibility.

Actual Family-context valid-use restrictions may apply without owning/redefining that vocabulary. Necessary revision of the governing responsibility mandate requires explicit assessed prior/new authority under§79.

**Current governing scope.** Exactly one primary responsibility is known for every applicable normalized document. Context may restrict valid use without redefining the four-value vocabulary or authorizing all pairs. Generic Type/kind examples and relationship types are separate from primary documentary Type;no automatic rekey on classification change.

---

## 7.3. Classification Classes

Class is a separate optional explicitly defined non-conflicting dimension;new defined values need actual responsible scope,not a universal Type-child level.

Any Class used shall respect its actual defined meaning and applicable semantic/structural scheme constraints,without mandatory third field,value or complete population being implied.

**Current governing scope.** Optional does not mean unrestricted. Actual category/value/definition/assignment and responsible scheme relation/meaning differ;no general maximum,mandatory third field or complete vocabulary is invented. Preserve known Schema34 duties despite genuine unrelated general-rule gaps.
