# 1. Introduction

Classification is the Documentation component defining semantic category meanings and applicable documentary assignments. It organizes logical documents as knowledge independently of physical location or a particular representation technology, while actual interfaces, sources and observations still matter.

This working Concepts edition defines scoped conceptual meanings and boundaries under current common Architecture, the adopted Review Policy and CLS-001 V2.0 R1. Its concepts are not a complete formal Model, accepted merged schema, populated category register or implemented classifier. Applicable normalized documents, pre-allocation subjects, category/definition entities, actual assignments, document editions and operational records are different subjects. Every README.md, CHANGELOG.md and ArchitecturalMilestones.md remains a non-normalized functional file.

The original Family/Type/hierarchy/evolution meanings are explicitly reconciled locally below; their exact received wording remains in history and external before/after maps, not simultaneously imposed as contradictory current definitions. Other exact owner sources and their actual discrepancies remain pending coordinated propagation (§44). No allocation, assignment, migration, formal approval, publication or Baseline selection is performed here.
