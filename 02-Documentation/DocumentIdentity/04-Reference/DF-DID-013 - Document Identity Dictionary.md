# 1. Purpose

This dictionary defines component-normative vocabulary for Document Identity within explicitly qualified subjects, domains and responsible contracts. The definitions contained herein are normative for the Document Identity component in that declared scope; this is not a claim of universal authority over every framework domain or of an approved implementation.

Common Architecture/Policy and responsible identity, descriptor, category, connection, registration, projection and lifecycle sources retain their applicable authority. DF-ARC-006 supplies owner-linked common consultation vocabulary and contextual aliases rather than replacing every detailed domain contract. Explicit applicable declarations, not Type Reference, a title or a repeated definition, establish normative authority.

The dictionary covers applicable assigned normalized logical documents and their identity-related meanings, while distinguishing pre-allocation subjects, files, exact editions/Status, mutable records and other entity domains. Every README.md, CHANGELOG.md and ArchitecturalMilestones.md remains non-normalized at every location. No identity, Metadata field/Profile, permission or operational service is created by naming a term.

# 2. Introduction

Interpret terms with their stated subject/domain/context, responsible source editions and controlled alias limits. Generic words such as Identity, Identifier, Lifecycle and Validation also have other entity-domain uses; documentary context does not erase those contracts.

Where common terminology and a component source appear to conflict, identify the actual responsible meaning, previous/current editions and affected consumers under common governing contracts. Preserve unresolved authority and narrower unambiguous observations; coordinated owner decisions and propagation remain necessary before broader conformity claims.

Canonical semantic meaning is distinct from document lifecycle Status, formal approval, publication or exact Baseline inclusion. ## 3.1. Assignment

**Definition**

In this documentary context, the governed act or event establishing an assigned qualified document key and its association with the intended applicable logical document under the responsible domain/allocation contract.

**Notes**

Assignment is a contextual short form of Identity Assignment (§3.11). One governing association is not recreated for every copy, content revision, registration or descriptor record. Pre-allocation and functional-file exclusions still apply.

The received Notes—occurs exactly once, creates the canonical identity, and does not imply approval or publication—are qualified as an actual responsible assignment decision/event, not automatic allocation from recognition or file creation. Actual authority/rules, subject/domain, collision/reservation/history and resulting association evidence are needed. No algorithm, user role or permission is supplied; assignment alone selects no Baseline member.

---

## 3.2. Canonical Identifier

**Definition**

The canonical identifier representation or interpretation used for an assigned identity under its governing domain contract; in documentary scope, a contextual role of Identifier (§3.21), not a second logical identity.

**Notes**

The four received qualities remain scoped:

| Quality | Qualified meaning / evidence boundary |
|---|---|
| Unique | Actual full qualified assignment/canonicalization within the governing domain and declared population/history |
| Permanent | Preserve same-document association, reservations and historical interpretation |
| Immutable | Assigned key continuity through ordinary evolution, not every supporting record |
| Referenceable | Intended qualified target/exact-edition interpretation; actual resolution and live availability require observations |

Canonical does not mean approved/published document Status or Baseline inclusion. Legitimate aliases/renderings require responsible mappings and do not alone allocate another key. See Domain (§3.12), Uniqueness (§3.28) and the corpus contract in §3.21.

## 3.3. Canonical Identity

**Definition**

The governing assigned document identity in its declared domain/canonical interpretation, contextually equivalent to Document Identity for the intended applicable assigned normalized logical document.

**Notes**

The received every-document/exactly-one statement applies to the governing document/domain assignment subject, not every physical file, pre-allocation subject, exact edition, category or other entity. Copies/aliases/editions are not automatically different logical identities. Canonical naming does not imply document Status=Canonical/Approved, publication or Baseline membership.

## 3.4. Document Identity

**Definition**

The enduring logical document identity under its applicable qualified Domain+Identifier allocation/preservation contract, associated with the same assigned normalized logical document through ordinary evolution.

**Notes**

The six received independence subjects remain:

- document content;
- repository;
- implementation;
- storage;
- governance.

These are semantic separation boundaries, not absence of actual contracts, decisions or evidence. Supporting descriptors/state/reference/index/projection records may change while the assigned key remains; logical representations/interfaces and actual required provider contracts still matter. Physical file, exact edition/Status and Baseline member are different subjects. Necessary governing-contract/representation transition follows §9; no arbitrary reassignment, mutation or global independence guarantee is introduced.

# 6. Naming Guidelines

The six original naming subjects remain, with qualified documentary usage rather than global renaming instructions:

- Use **Document Identity** for the assigned logical documentary identity under its responsible domain contract, distinct from rendering, file, edition and Status.
- Use **Identifier** for canonical representation/interpretation in an explicitly qualified domain; identify internal/file/database/tracking keys separately where they are different subjects.
- Use **Identity Domain** for governing qualification, allocation/canonicalization/preservation scope and owner rules, not a guaranteed collision-free label or mutable Family/Type.
- Use **Reference** with its actual endpoint type, direction/contract and intended target/context; document endpoints use qualified identity or exact-edition evidence, while other entities keep their contracts.
- Use **Assignment** for the actual initial governing association event in its intended documentary/domain scope, not every file creation, registration or support-record update.
- Use **Retirement** with event-versus-state context and actual end-of-active-use decision/scope; preserve same-document history/reservations and distinguish document Status/deletion from retirement.

Keep controlled aliases in §4, valid governed field names and qualified historical usage in §5. No naming recommendation grants operational permission, reassigns semantic ownership or creates a component, Metadata field/Profile, lifecycle state or document allocation.
