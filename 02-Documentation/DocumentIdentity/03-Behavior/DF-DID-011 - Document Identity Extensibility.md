# 1. Purpose

This document defines architectural requirements for extending and evolving Document Identity under applicable common Architecture/Policy and responsible domain contracts. It distinguishes ordinary compatible extension from necessary assessed normative/foundational revision. An extension label does not authorize changing owner meaning or prove backward compatibility.

Scope each proposal to actual intended subjects/domains, governing source editions and affected consumers. Applicable assigned normalized logical documents, pre-allocation subjects, other entity keys, physical files, exact editions/status and mutable supporting records are distinct. Every README.md, CHANGELOG.md and ArchitecturalMilestones.md remains non-normalized at every location; no identity is allocated merely because a file, category, representation or extension exists.

This source specifies documentary contracts and evidence boundaries, not implementation mechanisms, operational permissions, actual allocation/domain registration, installed adapters or a complete required graph. A local source correction/validation does not establish integrated conformity, formal architectural approval, publication or exact Baseline inclusion.

# 4. Extensibility Principles

The five original principle headings remain. They guide ordinary compatible extensions and separately assessed necessary normative revision under common governing contracts; no new AP identifiers are allocated here.

## 4.1. Backward Compatibility

Ordinary compatible extensions shall preserve the applicable meanings and assigned same-document key association of existing identities under their governing contracts. Compatibility requires actual prior/new provider/consumer/representation/reference assessment, not merely unchanged names or an additive label.

A necessary incompatible normative revision must follow §9 with explicit responsible authority, rationale/alternatives, affected exact owners/consumers/interfaces, mappings/history, Version/Revision and transition checks. It is not silently treated as a compatible extension. Preserve allocated/retired reservations, intended historical interpretation and immutable snapshot members; no arbitrary key reassignment or universal guarantee of live reference validity follows.

---

## 4.2. Canonical Preservation

An ordinary implementation extension shall not silently redefine responsible canonical document-key/domain semantics. Document Identity remains the semantic owner under applicable common Architecture/Policy, while other descriptor/category/connection/registration/projection owners retain their contracts.

The canonical model is an architectural contract, distinct from mutable support records and runtime representations. Necessary assessed normative/foundational revision follows common evolution principles and §9; core definition is not made unchangeable for all future governed editions merely by calling it canonical.

DID-014§14 still states additive/backward-compatible-only extensions and that core identity semantics are not extensible. Preserve that actual peer restriction and its pending owner/authority reconciliation with the controlled-evolution approach; this source-local correction neither waives it nor claims universal agreement or implementation permission.

---

## 4.3. Non-Destructive Evolution

Prefer additive, compatible evolution where it meets the actual need without competing semantics or unnecessary disruption. Preserve original evidence, assignments/reservations, same-document continuity and historical exact-snapshot interpretation.

Additive-only is not an absolute ban on a necessary normative/foundational revision under ARC-002 AP-015–018. Such revision requires §9 prior/new rules, responsible authority, alternatives/rationale, exact owner/consumer/required-interface impact, compatibility/version and transition evidence. No extension may silently invalidate governing assignments, rewrite historic failed checks or claim completed consumer propagation without evidence.
