# 1. Purpose

This document defines identity-governance requirements and responsibility/evidence boundaries for applicable assigned normalized logical documents under explicit identity-domain contracts. It governs the meaning and preservation of assigned keys and their same-document association, not the operation of repositories, access systems, approval engines or a universal workflow.

Distinguish identity-domain rules, common Architecture/Policy constraints, actual decisions under applicable authority, operational execution and observed results. Governance is the area comprising Workflow, Policies, Reviews and Baselines; those components define documentary contracts. DMS, tools or an applicable manual process execute operations, and CMS governs changes in its scope. Area/component registration is not evidence of an installed executor or a permission granted to act.

All files named README.md, CHANGELOG.md or ArchitecturalMilestones.md are non-normalized at every location. They require no normalized ID/header solely because they are files; applicable content, references, history and hash checks remain. Pre-allocation subjects, assigned qualified keys, mutable support records, physical representations, exact editions and other entity-domain identities are different subjects.

This source creates no user/permission model, approval process, repository policy or operational service. Identity requirements, received Canonical naming and local correction validation do not establish actual allocation, global conformity, formal approval, publication or Baseline membership.

# 2. Introduction

Document Identity supplies identity meaning for the actual scoped consumers that require it. It is not an assertion that every framework component depends on every identity contract or must route through a Registry/Graph service. Required consumer→provider uses identify actual contract, scope and responsible exact editions/interfaces under ARC-004/005; conceptual reference, authority, projection and execution are distinct.

For an applicable assigned normalized logical document, the qualified Domain+Identifier remains associated with the same document under its governing allocation/preservation contract. An exact edition, alias, file, index record or graph projection is not automatically another document identity. Unassigned concerns pre-allocation, not an allocated key that simultaneously does not exist.

Governance preserves responsible meaning, integrity, reservations and traceable decisions through actual document/record evolution. It does not mean that every supporting state/descriptor/reference record is immutable or that rule definitions themselves can never undergo assessed change. Applicable decisions need actual subject, rules/source editions, authority, executor, affected records/context and observations; this document does not invent a person, role assignment or approval solely from authorship or custody.

# 4. Governance Principles

The six original principle headings remain. Apply them to the declared subject/domain and governing rules; they do not allocate new framework AP identifiers or silently override common Architecture/Policy or other semantic owners.

---

## 4.1. Permanence

Preserve the assigned key's association with the same logical document under its governing domain contract. Retirement ends active use within its stated scope without releasing the allocated key for another document. Preserve historical association, reservations and intended reference interpretation; permanence does not guarantee live content or universally valid connections.

---

## 4.2. Immutability

Immutability concerns the assigned Domain+Identifier key and same-document association, not every state/reference/descriptor/registration/projection record. Ordinary content, metadata, classification, document-status, edition, repository and format changes preserve that key while their own records may evolve under responsible owners.

If a necessary authorized migration would alter an otherwise immutable representation/allocation contract, first reconcile prior/new owner rules, actual authority, affected exact sources/consumers and dependencies, old→new mappings, aliases/reservations/collisions/history, target and exact-snapshot continuity, compatibility and transition checks. Never reassign an allocated/retired key to another document or retarget immutable historical Baseline members. A governance/validation label does not authorize mutation; this UPDATE executes no migration.

---

## 4.3. Uniqueness

An applicable assigned normalized logical document has one governing identity under its declared document/domain contract. Pre-allocation subjects, excluded functional files, other entity kinds and exact editions are not additional required DF document keys.

Uniqueness concerns full qualified Domain+Identifier, actual canonical/alias interpretation and scoped assignment/population/history. Multiple copies, repeated registrations, graph projections or editions about one logical key are not by themselves duplicate allocations. Actual competing document assignments or incompatible canonical interpretations need observed evidence under the responsible rules.

For this corpus, the domain is Documentation Framework, represented by DF. DF-SSS-NNN uses a registered uppercase three-letter stable allocation series and positive three-digit001–999 allocation. Current Family/Type/Component/path/edition/status do not regenerate the key; equal numeric suffixes across different full series identifiers do not alone collide. 000 is excluded; gaps prove no availability; allocated/retired numbers remain reserved. At999, register a traceable unused continuation series rather than widen or recycle silently. DID-005§4.2 and Policy§4 govern detailed corpus rules; external domains need their own explicit contracts.

Record actual domain/series authority, collision/reservation/current-historical population checks and coverage. Syntax success or registration alone proves no unique allocation or global resolver.

---

## 4.4. Non-Reusability

Allocated and retired keys remain reserved against assignment to a different logical document. Actual retirement scope/decision, reservation history and same-document association must remain traceable. Retirement neither erases prior identity evidence nor guarantees all indexes were updated or content remains retrievable.

A gap, missing record or absent live content does not establish that a number is available. Distinguish observed reassignment from missing history; retain actual violations and evidence rather than rewriting the past. This source allocates, retires and reassigns no key.

---

## 4.5. Traceability

Preserve assignment, evolution, retirement, rule/decision and reference provenance for the intended qualified document or exact edition/context. Long-term historical interpretation is a requirement; actual target validity, freshness and current/future live retrieval remain separately observed facts.

A valid document-targeting reference identifies its intended existing qualified logical document or exact edition with the additional coordinates/bytes and context needed. Other endpoints follow their own contracts; optional incoming0..N does not require a reference merely for a key to exist. Keep dangling, ambiguous, stale and unavailable observations explicit. Never silently create or retarget a key to make a reference appear valid, and never retarget immutable historical snapshot members.

---

## 4.6. Independence

The six original independence subjects remain: document content, metadata, governance workflows, repositories, implementation technologies and storage mechanisms. Ordinary changes there do not automatically redefine or reallocate identity. This is semantic separation, not absence of actual evidence, interactions, owner requirements or operational decisions.

Identity governance may inspect relevant content, descriptors, representations, state/decision records, registrations and references without taking over their complete validation or semantics. Logical representation/interface and adapter requirements can be legitimate while storage/runtime choices remain open. Required contracts, including actual reciprocal Metadata/Identity claims, must not be hidden by an independence or projection label.

# 6. Governance Rules

## 6.1. Rule 1

Require one governing assigned identity for an applicable assigned normalized logical document under its declared contract. Apply functional-file exclusions and distinguish pre-allocation, files, exact editions, copies and other entity identities. No allocation occurs merely to satisfy a blanket “every document” assertion.

Decision/check evidence identifies actual subject, domain and assignment history, exact artifacts/context and coverage; recognition or registration alone is not completed allocation.

---

## 6.2. Rule 2

Preserve the assigned qualified key and same-document association through ordinary evolution under governing rules. Supporting lifecycle, reference, descriptor, index and graph records may legitimately change. Any necessary contract/representation migration first requires §4.2 prior/new owner/authority/consumer/history and snapshot-continuity assessment; no arbitrary key change or evidence rewrite is authorized.

---

## 6.3. Rule 3

Prevent conflicting allocations/canonical interpretations of a full qualified key for different logical documents within its actual governed domain. Do not classify every copy, repeated record, alias or exact edition as a new duplicate identity. Actual population, representation/alias rules, observed collisions and reservation/history coverage determine the scoped claim.

---

## 6.4. Rule 4

Do not reassign an allocated key to a different logical document, whether active or retired. Preserve original same-document association and history. Legitimate observations/records or governed representations of the same key do not create a new allocation; responsible prior/new contract assessment remains required before any necessary migration.

---

## 6.5. Rule 5

Retain retired-key reservations and historical association with the intended logical document under the applicable domain contract. Record actual retirement decision/scope, assignment/reservation evidence and coverage. Missing history or unavailable content is not permission to reuse a number, nor automatically proof that a particular reassignment happened.

---

## 6.6. Rule 6

For each applicable document-targeting reference, preserve intended existing qualified document or exact-edition/context interpretation under the responsible connection/endpoint contract. Other endpoint kinds use their own identity rules and incoming population may be0..N.

Actual reference validity and retrieval need observed evidence; no perpetual guarantee follows from stable identity. Never silently create or retarget an identity or immutable historical member to resolve a reference. Connection records remain with their responsible owner; this rule does not acquire all relation semantics.

---

## 6.7. Rule 7

Document Identity owns document identity semantics; other components shall not silently redefine them. Applicable Foundation/Architecture/Policy constraints and responsible domain contracts still govern their declared scopes. Definition ownership does not override those sources, grant operational authority or make one document the sole source of every rule.

Metadata descriptors, Classification categories, Relationships connections, Registry registration and Knowledge Graph projections retain their semantic/record owners. Implementations preserve governed identity meaning and may use legitimate representations/adapters without creating competing identity semantics. Actual required consumer→provider contracts and unresolved cycles remain visible; data projection does not remove an indispensable contract.

# 10. Governance Violations

The six original violation subjects remain, now tied to actual governing rule/subject/context and observed evidence. A condition is an actual violation only when the applicable requirement and observed nonconformance are established; suspected problems, missing history or unresolved owner authority are recorded with those limits rather than fabricated into failures or success.

| Received violation subject | Actual violating meaning / legitimate distinction |
|---|---|
| Modifying an assigned identity | Unauthorized or nonconforming change of the governed key/same-document association; ordinary mutable support records are not all key modifications, and a necessary contract migration requires prior/new reconciliation before application |
| Assigning multiple identities to the same document | Competing allocations contrary to the applicable document/domain/canonical contract; exact editions, aliases or representations are not automatically different logical identities |
| Assigning the same identity to multiple documents | Observed conflicting assignment of a full qualified key to different logical documents in the governed domain; equal numeric suffixes across different series or copies of one document are not alone a violation |
| Reusing a retired identity | Actual reassignment of a reserved key to a different document; missing records or live content do not establish availability |
| Creating implementation-specific identity semantics | Changing responsible logical meaning through implementation; legitimate domain-preserving adapters/representations and applicable interface constraints are not automatically violations |
| Bypassing canonical identity definitions | Actual circumvention of governing responsible rules; common Architecture/Policy and other domain owners remain applicable, and actual peer authority conflicts must be reconciled rather than ignored |

For an observed or suspected issue:

1. Preserve exact original artifacts/records, applicable rules/editions, intended subject/domain/context, observations and coverage; state whether a violation is established or evidence/authority is missing.
2. Identify responsible semantic owners and actual decision/execution authority for the proposed correction. A review finding, governance label or validation result alone grants no mutation, allocation, operational permission, approval or publication.
3. Before a necessary normative change or identity/path/representation migration, assess prior/new contract, rationale, affected owners/consumers and actual required dependencies; record compatibility, mappings, reservations/history/aliases/reference and exact-snapshot continuity plus transition checks.
4. Execute only the separately authorized scoped change with actual contributor/event provenance; preserve historical failed checks and original evidence. No automatic repair or silent target/identity creation is performed by governance evaluation.
5. Validate the exact changed artifacts/editions in the appropriate subsequent operation and report coverage/remaining conflicts. Do not rewrite an earlier failure or pending event as if it had always passed.

For changed working documents, compatible correction retains Version; compatible additive capability increments minor; incompatible contract change increments major and resets minor to0.
